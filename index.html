About

I am a Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Techno-Functional Consultant who has more than 24 years of experience in the design, configuration, implementation, upgrade, and integration of ERP applications within Finance, Cost Management, Distribution, Manufacturing, Retail, and Warehouse Automation areas.

Experience in delivering ERP projects for both Dynamics 365 Business Central SaaS and On-Prem platforms, migrations from Dynamics NAV to Business Central, financial localization (IFRS, US GAAP, AU GST, EU VAT), and enterprise integration using new API technologies following Microsoft best practices.

Hands-on experience in developing customizations on an extension basis, integrating automated solutions for warehouses and manufacturing processes in Dynamics NAV/Business Central.

Extensive experience in working with Visual Studio Code, AL Extensions, .NET framework, Microsoft Azure (Entra), Microsoft Power Platform (Power BI Reports, Power Automate), Microsoft SQL Server, and SharePoint.

Communication and interpersonal skills allowing effective collaboration with stakeholders and business users to collect their requirements for the project. Experience in demonstrating ERP systems and solutions to customers, leading user training, and discussing possible solutions together with business.
